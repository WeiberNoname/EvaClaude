# Chapter 01 implementation sequence

Approved direction: the condensed first encounter, extended with a building-supplied heavy cannon, a shoulder-stowed progressive knife, and power stations. These weapon provisions are gameplay adaptations, not a claim of a shot-for-shot television reconstruction.

1. Playable first-person arrival: telephone, distant Sachiel, attack, Misato's car.
2. Short rescue transition and underground hangar: Gendo's demand, Shinji's refusal, injured Rei, Unit-01's protective movement.
3. Entry-plug sequence and vertical launch; switch from human scale to Eva controls.
4. Battle systems: humanoid Sachiel, opening armory doors and rising cannon rack, physical weapon pickup, visible shoulder-knife extraction, charging stations and tether.
5. Escalation: player breaches the field and damages Sachiel; signal loss, autonomous Unit-01 counterattack, explosion, hospital dialogue, chapter results.
6. Validate rules, run the chapter and equipment interactions end to end, inspect rendered scenes, rebuild the standalone Windows package and retain Play EVA.

Presentation uses authored captions, procedural characters, component animation, synthesized effects, and scripted cameras. Full voice acting and production character meshes are outside this implementation.

Implementation status: all six steps are implemented in the chapter prototype. Three rules tests and the packaged rendered chapter/equipment/checkpoint integration run passed on September 19, 2026. See VERIFICATION.md for evidence and remaining production limitations.
