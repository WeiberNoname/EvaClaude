# Six Evangelion-inspired additions

This pass uses the requested films as art direction. The mechanics are original gameplay interpretations, not a claim to reproduce every film's rules or scenes exactly.

1. **New: synchronization overdrive.** Z requires 80 synchronization and eight reserve seconds. It spends 20 synchronization, grants 60% more core damage for eight seconds, and drains an extra 2.5 reserve seconds per second. Cooldown is 24 seconds. Successful hits rebuild synchronization.
2. **New: anti-A.T. field pulse.** X neutralizes an active enemy barrier within 32 metres for eight seconds. It costs 25 synchronization and 12 reserve seconds, with an 18-second cooldown. It does not directly damage the core.
3. **Improved: cannon charge.** Left-click fires normally. In v0.6, hold middle-click for up to two seconds and release for increased barrier and core damage; right-click aims. Full charge costs one shell and eight reserve seconds, breaches a full field, and deals 319 damage to an already exposed core before overdrive. Camera zoom and a capacitor meter communicate charging. Cooldown, reload, and insufficient-power rejections preserve resources; aimed misses and impacts on cover spend a shell.
4. **Improved: umbilical feedback.** A distance meter, red cable, and warning at 85% reach make the tether limit visible. Exceeding 62 metres allows two seconds to retreat before disconnecting. Returning inside the limit clears strain. Reconnecting restores external power.
5. **Style: stepped armor shading.** A quantized normal-based key-light ramp emphasizes broad color areas while retaining dynamic shadows and neon details. The v0.6 armor uses separate roughness/metallic parameters and brighter fill light to keep the purple panels readable.
6. **Style: command displays.** Red/black alert plates, warning strips, synchronization readouts, and prominent power and attack warnings reinforce the anime control-room presentation. Captions and Third Impact letterboxing remain readable.

Third Impact is a separate one-minute cinematic with a direct launcher. These mechanics apply to the playable first-encounter battle and optional free-roam encounters. Shamshel joins Sachiel in v0.6. Faceted component models and synthesized sound remain prototypes for production assets.
